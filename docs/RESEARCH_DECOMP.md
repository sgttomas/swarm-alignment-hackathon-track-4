## 2. Inquiry directions and important distinctions

The four directions below remain available and can overlap. They are not package
boundaries or four required experiments. The Resources tab supplies additional
angles that may reshape them as evidence and feasibility become clearer.

Use the PRD's working framing—oversight under incomplete, unreliable, or distributed
evidence—to develop candidate questions. Each case must explain the interacting-agent
mechanism that makes it a Track 4 contribution and the oversight decision it could
inform. The philosophical perspectives help expose relevant distinctions; the
case still needs its own scientific grounds and relationship to prior work.

| Direction | Possible questions and source leads | Examination issue to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Coordination and information spread | Real swarm forensics, partial-message monitoring, gateway observations, covert communication, shared-prior coordination | Distinguish observed interaction from inferred coordination, legitimate cooperation from the selected failure, and missing observation from absence |
| Completion reports and actual outcomes | Compare reported actions with independently observed state; investigate propagation of false completion reports | Bind state and observation time; distinguish a false report from delayed state, a failed query, or an incorrect reference |
| Failure diagnosis and evaluator reliability | Fault attribution, planted errors, wrong references or graders, monitor persuasion, disagreement among replicas | Preserve competing explanations, information access, false accusations, missed failures, and insufficient-evidence outcomes |
| Control of cooperating agents | Trusted-monitor variants, explicit communication channels, governance roles, sandbagging, distributed research sabotage, fragmented approvals | Evaluate the combined behavior and enforced versus instructional limits; examine usefulness and control burden as well as the selected failure |

Case definitions must distinguish a question from a hypothesis; an agent report
from an observation; a finding from its explanation; an intervention from its
measured effect; and a research result from acceptance or publication. Terms such
as collusion, sabotage, success, and independence need definitions where they
change an examination. These working distinctions remain open to refinement.

### Research route follows the question

| Route | Possible contribution | What must be examined |
|---|---|---|
| Existing records | New forensic findings, systematic observations, offline monitor evaluation, or comparison of retained cases | Access, source selection, coverage, labels and missing information; causal claims require a defensible identification argument |
| Controlled experiments | New observations about a selected intervention or behavior under specified conditions | Actual control/manipulation, comparability, sampling, reference validity, observer access, costs, and limits of transfer |
| Theory, methodology, or synthesis | An examined argument, counterexample, evaluation method, or substantive integration of existing evidence | Definitions, premises, inference, contrary sources/cases, applicability, and what the contribution adds |

Routes can be combined. New live-agent trials are not a universal event
requirement or a prerequisite for DEL-01-02. A literature review must have a
clear purpose and grounds for its own synthesis; a summary alone should not be
presented as a newly established empirical result. Select the route and necessary
support from the question rather than assuming an experimental runtime must be built.

### DEL-01-01 — Research basis and bounded case definitions

**Purpose and coverage:** Turn selected Track 4 leads into useful, examinable
questions while keeping alternatives visible. Supports O1/O2/O5 and R05,
R13, R16–R19, R22.

**Inputs and conditions:** Owner direction, PRD, all four event tabs, relevant
primary literature and prior work, and observations about available data and
tools. Website descriptions identify leads; examine the actual source before a
dependent novelty, method, or feasibility claim.

**Outputs and approach:** A concise current research basis and case briefs, with
the question, intended use, prior work, definitions, assumptions, proposed method,
needed inputs, allowed variation, resource conditions, and examination route.
Each brief identifies its research route, the contribution it could make, and
whether existing evidence can support the intended inference. Check actual data
access and suitability before depending on a named dataset; retain an alternative
route or a scoped limitation if that condition cannot be established.
Empirical cases identify relevant comparison/sampling and information-access
conditions; theoretical cases identify premises and the argument to examine.
Specific thresholds and repetitions require grounds. The final paper's hypothesis
and every exploratory branch need not be fixed at once.

**Ownership and examination:** HELPS_HUMANS supports design with the participant;
one case-definition owner reconciles criticism and revisions. Scrutiny asks whether
the proposed observations or argument could address the question, expose a competing
explanation, and justify the intended inference. Counterexamples, flawed oracles,
and infeasible input conditions can require redesign before dependent work.

**Completion and limits:** A selected brief is usable by its producer and reviewer,
with unresolved conditions tied to the work they affect. Discovery of an unproductive
lead can justify retaining or ending it with reasons. This deliverable does not
promise a literature census or commission all candidate cases.

Its initial formation work and concrete output conditions are defined in §6.1.
Those conditions distinguish a usable research proposition from a topic list or
an already completed planning document.

### DEL-01-02 — Investigations and supported findings

**Purpose and coverage:** Carry each selected case through actual inquiry, analysis,
critique, repair, and a bounded conclusion. Supports O1/O2/O5 and R06, R13,
R15–R19, R21–R22.

**Inputs and conditions:** The identified case basis from DEL-01-01, suitable
observations and operations from PKG-02 where needed, actual execution authority
and resources, and the selected review arrangement. A case that needs no new
software can use adequate existing means directly. A case using retained records
does not need a new agent run to count as an empirical investigation.

**Outputs and approach:** Inspectable observations or derivations, analysis and
case findings with assumptions, counterevidence, uncertainty, limitations, and
review dispositions. Bind evidence to the actual configuration and question.
For selected comparisons, retain unsuccessful and interrupted attempts and the
reason for exclusions. Distinguish exploratory discovery from later confirmation.
Report relevant failure rates, errors, uncertainty, or burden only where the case
and observations support those quantities.

**Ownership and examination:** The investigation owner retains the case through
repair; WORKING_ITEMS can coordinate several contributors. The examiner receives
the actual candidate and relevant grounds. Scrutiny challenges inference, evaluation
quality, alternative causes, information leakage, and applicability. Corrections
are backchecked and propagated to DEL-03-01 when they change the account.

**Completion and limits:** The selected investigation and its required examination
are fulfilled, with a conclusion no stronger than its grounds. Negative or
inconclusive findings are valid when adequately established. A broken instrument,
missing required observation, or unperformed check can instead leave the case
incomplete. Each case is bounded by steering; this is not an unlimited research program.

### 6.1 Formation: develop an answerable and useful inquiry

Examine a bounded selection of the closest primary work and obtainable evidence.
Inspect source contents or representative data where permitted; endpoint reachability
alone does not establish a useful dataset. Compare plausible questions across the
open directions without assuming all must become studies. A small extraction,
observation, or analytical probe is appropriate when it resolves a named uncertainty.
An extended general platform or confirmatory campaign is premature until its case
and necessary conditions are supported.

For each serious candidate, produce a concise case brief containing:

- **Problem, recipient, and multi-agent mechanism:** the oversight decision or
  difficulty, who would use the result, the relevant agents/principals and their
  interactions, and what those interactions could change relative to an isolated
  worker's performance. Whether the hypothesized difference occurs is for the
  investigation to establish.
- **Prior work and possible contribution:** actual examined sources, what they
  already establish, the proposed addition or challenge, and limits of the novelty
  search. Identify the nearest organizer example and its cited work; state what
  this case would add, test, replicate usefully, or challenge, and the extra cost
  of that contribution. An organizer example or source list alone does not
  substantiate novelty. Apply the same event rubric to every research route.
- **Research question and alternatives:** hypotheses or competing explanations
  for an empirical comparison, or the descriptive question/theoretical proposition
  and premises for another route. No statistical hypothesis is required where it
  would misrepresent the contribution.
- **Observation and inference conditions:** the study unit and scope; actor,
  monitor, and evaluator access; assumptions about state, identity, timing, or
  ground truth; and what observations or arguments could discriminate among the
  alternatives. Identify meaningful negative, contrary, or inconclusive outcomes.
- **Feasibility and method:** access actually established, source/data coverage,
  relevant usage conditions, the intended examination, and unresolved conditions
  that affect it. Distinguish retained-record analysis, controlled trials, and
  argument/synthesis, including what each route cannot establish.
- **Means, effort, and scrutiny:** existing operations to reuse, the smallest
  justified support gap, relevant resources and limits, review/backcheck route,
  and time needed for analysis and reporting. Keep uncertain costs explicit and
  identify any choice outside the current grant.

### 6.2 Focused investigation: produce and examine findings

Proceed with a case whose necessary conditions and authority are established,
while other cases may remain in formation. Develop the detailed protocol or
argument and the minimum support needed for it. An early representative case
should exercise observation or derivation, analysis, critique, repair, and return
before multiplying a fragile premise across many runs or contributors.

Identify criteria and comparison conditions before relying on a confirmatory
test. Preserve which observations informed the hypothesis, and examine selection
effects when reusing them. Match sampling, controls, validation, or proof scrutiny
to the claim. The actual research route can use existing records without new
agent trials. A working collector or runner establishes a support capability;
the investigator still has to establish what follows from its outputs.

Return to formation for the affected question if a source, measurement, evaluator,
access condition, or result undermines the intended inference. Preserve the prior
basis and useful evidence. Narrowing a claim, changing route, or ending a branch
can be warranted; a new hypothesis does not retrospectively become the one tested.
Continue unaffected authorized work and apply the established review/repair cycle.

### 6.3 Synthesis: establish the contribution and communicate it

Develop the report outline and prior-work account early. As findings arrive,
examine the combined argument, source compatibility, new inferences, contrary
results, uncertainty, and limits. Route gaps or overstatements to their owners
and backcheck the repaired account. Report what the work adds, including a
well-supported limitation or inconclusive finding, without overstating coverage.

Before returning the selected report candidate, check the actual PDF and required
metadata against the event requirements. Preserve evidence needed to inspect its
claims. A complete-looking report cannot cure missing research grounds; unfinished
work remains explicit even though the event permits its submission. External
submission follows the owner's actual direction.

## 7. Examination of this proposal

The author checked scope and requirement coverage and walked the following
constructed cases through the proposed responsibilities. These are design

| Case | What the proposal must support |
|---|---|
| A completion claim disagrees with state | DEL-01-01 defines the relevant state/time; DEL-02-01 qualifies the observation; DEL-01-02 examines competing explanations; DEL-03-01 reports only the supported finding |

bounds practical; the structure alone does not enforce time or produce quality.
