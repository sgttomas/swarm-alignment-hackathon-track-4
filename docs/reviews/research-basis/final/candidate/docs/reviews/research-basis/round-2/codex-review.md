The November 8 18:00 UTC draft and 23:00 UTC checked-candidate targets leave 17 hours 59 minutes and 12 hours 59 minutes respectively before the official November 9 11:59 UTC cutoff. That is usable recovery margin. Neither these targets nor the two-hour interval proves sufficient participant or reviewer capacity; activation must reconcile the actual start and availability as the candidate already requires. I found no conflicting promise to finish the entire inquiry within the first pass.

**Resource conditions.** The parent is right to reject deciding every spend/access question before any formation. A primary-source comparison, an argument's premises, and a public-record feasibility check can provide decision value while a controlled-trial route remains conditional. Formation can estimate what a trial would need without claiming that its resources are available. Requiring all possible accounts first could itself spend owner attention and steer the inquiry toward unnecessary acquisitions.

I also retain the consequential part of Claude's objection: do not wait until the final recommendation to reveal a known lead-time dependency. AI Village's manual gate and possible model costs are already known. At activation, identify which serious leads depend on them; promptly present the specific choice, implications of delay, and an available alternative. This is required behavior under the revised text, not grounds for another blanket gate. New authority must not be inferred from sponsor credits being advertised.

**Scientific contribution and event fit.** I re-extracted relevant passages from the retained raw panels. The completion/state and failure-attribution examples closely match the owner's directions. A central future check remains whether the case's interaction mechanism adds an actual multi-agent question.
