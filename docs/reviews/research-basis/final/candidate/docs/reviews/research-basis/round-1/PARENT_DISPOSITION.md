## Parent assessment to carry into convergence

The separation between research definition, evidence access, analysis support,
investigation, and synthesis remains useful. The obligations can be initialized
without selecting a model, hypothesis, dataset, favorable result, or full platform.
Formation should consume the smallest suitable source and observation set that
supports a real choice, and the first-return bound must not substitute for that
scientific work.

For setup, the proposed mapping needs to state what PROJECT means here: adoption
of the current execution-locality and SOW/native-needs contracts as a consumer
profile. It must not claim that the research decomposition was produced by the
project-decomp workflow or retroactively satisfied all of that method's invariants.
The source scope, package-purpose/objective references, actual accepted portions,
and conditional needs must remain readable and traceable without a new schema.

No requirement for fresh agent trials, a finished question before formation, a
registered method before explicitly selected draft use, or a new general
orchestrator is justified by the completed review. The final recommendation is
subject to both reviewers' examination of the revised candidate below.
