# Research decomposition walkthroughs

These are constructed examples for examining the method. They are neither
research results nor a required sequence of investigations. Use a relevant case
to challenge a proposed decomposition, adapting its details to the undertaking.

## Broad exploration with shared means

A participant wants to investigate coordination, unreliable completion reports,
failure diagnosis, and control mechanisms without selecting a final paper yet.
Several investigations could use the same source collector and state inspection.

A useful decomposition identifies the authorized exploratory scope and the
candidate questions separately. It gives a shared capability an intended use and
an owning contribution, and states which investigations need which part of it.
A bounded feasibility probe can determine whether the capability is worth
building. The four questions do not automatically become four promised studies.

**Examine:** Can one useful case start while another lacks data? Are the source
access and output conditions adequate for that case? Would the proposed common
tool have a consumer if one research direction ended? Is an optional branch
mistaken for a commitment merely because it has a place in the account?

## An agent's completion report differs from state

A proposed study asks whether checking a worker's completion claim before another
worker acts reduces downstream mistakes. The case uses a controlled state store.
No behavior or improvement has yet been observed.

The contribution account identifies the claim, the relevant state and observation
time, the proposed intervention, and the intended comparison. It distinguishes
what each worker can access, what the monitor can observe at its decision point,
and what the evaluator may inspect afterward. The state query and reference
answer need their own examination. A later state change or a faulty reference
can produce an apparent false completion without establishing one.

Possible connected contributions include qualifying the observation route,
defining and running a bounded comparison, and analyzing its outcomes and burden.
They need not become three separate deliverables if one owner can carry a
coherent, examinable contribution. Instrumentation is judged by the observations
it faithfully supports; the intervention is judged by the outcomes it changes.

**Examine:** Does the proposed evidence distinguish the competing explanations?
Could an ordinary successful run be falsely classified? Does the monitor rely on
evaluation-only information? Do the intended comparison and scope justify any
later causal claim? Can a null or adverse result still be reported usefully?

## Inconclusive result versus unfinished investigation

One study completes its selected examination but cannot distinguish the competing
explanations within the observations and uncertainty it obtained. Another stops
before obtaining the required observations because its collector fails.

The first may satisfy a contribution that promises an adequate investigation and
an honestly bounded conclusion. Its interpretation still needs to establish why
the evidence is inconclusive; absence of a detected effect does not establish
equivalence. The second has unfinished work unless its actual scope was a
feasibility probe whose result the failure establishes. A label cannot erase a
promised examination, and spending the budget alone establishes neither quality
nor completion.

**Examine:** Are the original promises and criteria recoverable? Is inability to
perform the study distinguished from a supported scientific conclusion? Does a
remaining obligation have a useful next action, an actual scope decision, or an
explicit limitation on reliance?

## A theoretical contribution and conflicting definitions

Two sources use "coordination" differently. A participant proposes an argument
that a monitor with a particular observation boundary cannot distinguish two
classes of behavior.

Retain the sources' definitions, their applicability, and the proposed definition
used by this argument. The investigation identifies its premises and observation
model, constructs the relevant cases, and examines the reasoning and possible
counterexamples. A literature corpus, implementation benchmark, or numerical
score is not automatically required. A new definition or theorem remains a
proposal until its grounds are examined; an accurate source quotation alone does
not prove it. The conclusion applies only under the conditions the argument uses.

**Examine:** Is the contribution an argument that can be checked? Do its premises
describe the intended subject? Could an omitted channel invalidate it? Would a
finding change the question's framing or only the argument under that framing?

## A changed criterion after exploratory work

Early cases suggest a new failure mode and a more useful measure. The participant
wants to pursue that direction using some of the existing observations.

Preserve the previous question, criteria, and results. Identify the new question
and which work or interpretation changes. Reused observations can support
exploration, with that reuse disclosed. A confirmatory or generalization claim
requires an appropriate subsequent evaluation design, including separation of
development and evaluation information where needed. A numerical target chosen
after seeing results must not be presented as an unchanged prior criterion.

**Examine:** Does ordinary refinement remain possible within the authorized
scope? Are changed commitments returned to their actual decision owner? Can
unaffected work continue? Are discarded explanations and unsuccessful attempts
retained where the resulting claim depends on their selection history?

## Interruption, missing history, and downstream setup

A contributor returns an output but its execution record is incomplete. On
resumption, the available instructions are newer than those governing the run.
The recipient also wants to prepare formal work localities from the decomposition.

Inspect the actual saved result and recover the earlier basis where possible.
State missing context and how it limits attribution or comparison. Do not replace
the earlier basis with current instructions or repeat an uncertain write without
checking its outcome. An unaffected contribution can continue within authority.

For setup, bind the accepted decomposition's actual portions, identities,
outputs, evaluation conditions, and consuming input needs to the selected setup
method. Shared identifier shapes help preserve identity but cannot establish
schema or semantic compatibility. A required adapter is explicit work. A reader
can use the contribution account directly if the assignment needs no automated
setup route; the method does not invent a supported research schema or create
an execution tree to make a check appear successful.

**Examine:** Can the next owner locate what exists, what was checked, what remains
unknown, and the next permitted action? Does the handoff distinguish accepted
scope from proposed or deferred work? Does its claimed downstream compatibility
have evidence beyond matching names and paths?

## A critique changes the reported result

A candidate analysis claims that a monitor improves outcomes. A reviewer reads
the actual case records and finds that failed trials were excluded and the
reference checker used a stale state snapshot. The contribution owner checks
both findings, corrects the analysis, and qualifies the checker. The revised
comparison no longer establishes the claimed improvement.

An adequate return preserves the original comparison basis, the findings and
their disposition, and the actual revised evidence. The backcheck examines the
affected trials and a legitimate success case, then follows the changed analysis
into the synthesis and report. A supported narrower conclusion is an improvement
in the research result even when the proposed monitor performs less favorably.

A further critic objection turns out to concern a different candidate revision.
The owner demonstrates that mismatch rather than changing correct work. Review
findings and their proposed repairs both need grounds.

**Examine:** Is there a named repair owner and an actual backcheck? Does the final
report reflect the revised result? Are author checks, independent scrutiny, and
independent evidence described accurately? Could a source-research packet support
the experimental conclusion by itself, or are new observations still required?

## Formation before dependent tooling

A participant has several promising topics and a list of dataset links, but has
not examined the closest prior work or the data contents. A proposed generic
monitoring platform would consume substantial effort before any question is
shown to be answerable.

The decomposition defines an initial formation contribution: inspect relevant
primary sources and obtainable samples, develop candidate questions and competing
explanations, and recommend a feasible next investigation with its required means
and scrutiny. A small parsing probe is permitted where it tests a named access or
measurement condition. A full platform is not justified by the topic list alone.

Suppose the records omit identity information needed by one attribution question.
That candidate remains conditional or is reframed. Another question about observable
temporal patterns may remain viable, but still needs its own inference and prior-work
examination. A theoretical candidate can proceed by inspecting premises and
counterexamples rather than acquiring an irrelevant dataset. A report outline and
source account can develop while these choices are investigated.

**Examine:** Does formation return a useful comparison and recommendation rather
than an indefinite reading exercise? Does a case progress only when its necessary
conditions and authority are established? Can one case move while others stay open?
Can a failed feasibility check produce an honest reframe without being labelled a
successful study? If no candidate is supported within the available bounds, is that
finding returned with its evidence and useful next action? Are phase changes kept
distinct from human decisions, staffing layers, and package boundaries?
