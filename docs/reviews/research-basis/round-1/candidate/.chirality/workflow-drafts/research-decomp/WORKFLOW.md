# Decompose a research undertaking

Use this method to organize an inquiry whose useful contributions can include
empirical investigation, theoretical argument, analysis, reusable methods, tools,
and synthesis. Develop enough structure for the next work to be carried out and
examined while preserving questions whose answers remain open. A commitment to
investigate does not promise a positive finding or a preferred explanation.

Use an existing adequate brief for a single bounded investigation. This method
is useful when several contributions, shared means, or unresolved choices need
to fit together. It defines the work and its relationships; detailed experimental
protocols, execution, and research conclusions belong to the resulting work.

## Responsibilities and authority

HELPS_HUMANS develops the research structure with the human. HELP_HUMAN retains
purpose and continuity; WORKING_ITEMS can carry the preparation and integration
when useful. One owner remains responsible for the assembled decomposition.
Roles describe contributions, not a required roster of separate agents. TASK
may perform a bounded source, design, or review assignment through its caller;
it does not select this workflow, own its human dialogue, or delegate.

The actual assignment supplies authority. Use decisions already given, and
prepare consequential choices concretely before returning them to the human.
Workflow selection, a proposed decomposition, and an available host capability
do not expand permissions or authorize research execution, release, or reliance.

## Establish the assignment and output location

Read the conversation, current project instructions, supplied research intent or
PRD, existing work, and relevant sources. Identify the recipient and intended use,
known constraints and resources, permitted writes, and the basis's standing.
Separate adopted direction from draft proposals, assumptions, and options.
An initial decomposition can be proposed from incomplete intent; identify which
parts still need a decision rather than treating the entire input as accepted.

Resolve the working root and output target from the assignment and project
conventions. Reuse an existing research/decomposition document when appropriate.
If none exists, propose `docs/RESEARCH_DECOMP.md` within the authorized working
root as the initial working document. Do not overwrite an accepted predecessor
or create an execution tree merely to satisfy this method. Retain the selected
workflow source and revision with the basis where their identity matters.

The normal output is one coherent decomposition account containing:

- purpose, boundaries, source basis and its standing;
- questions, objectives, uncertainties, and relevant definitions;
- bounded contributions with outputs, ownership, and examination conditions;
- actual input/use relationships and shared capabilities;
- progression from formation to investigation and synthesis, with the conditions
  needed by each dependent contribution and reasons to revisit the basis;
- coverage, consequential choices, open conditions, and review findings;
- the exact accepted portions and next authorized use, if acceptance occurs.

These are information requirements, not six mandatory files. Use tables or
companion registers only when a consumer benefits, with one owning location for
each declaration. Keep source material and evidence in their appropriate homes.

## 1. Develop the research basis

Explain what the inquiry is meant to help someone understand or do, why it
matters, and what would make its contribution useful. Relate it to relevant
existing work without inventing novelty, feasibility, or expected results.

Distinguish source statements, observations, arguments, hypotheses, candidate
interventions, adopted commitments, and open questions. Identify sources and
versions where differences matter. Check fidelity before relying on a summary,
transcription, or transformed dataset. Preserve material disagreements and the
conditions under which apparently conflicting claims might both apply.

Define ambiguous concepts where they affect investigation or interpretation.
Keep a source's taxonomy open to criticism; it supplies a possible organization
of the subject, not proof that it exhausts the subject. New concepts and
hypotheses are permitted when identified as proposals with their rationale.

Examine what the inquiry says exists, what supports its claims, how its work
can be carried through, and what matters to its participants and affected uses.
Ontology, epistemology, praxeology, and axiology are related questions here,
not mandatory packages, forms, or a framework-specific validity score.

Preserve several inquiry directions when useful. Mark which work is committed,
which is a candidate, which is excluded, and which remains undecided. A bounded
commitment to explore can be in scope while its eventual hypothesis or finding
remains unknown. Listing a promising question does not commission every possible
study of it. Identify constraints on exploration and when its direction should
be reconsidered, without inventing numerical targets or a fixed study count.

### Represent question formation as actual work

If intent is broad, define a bounded formation and feasibility contribution before
substantial dependent implementation or confirmatory work. Its purpose is to turn
the framing into researchable questions and plausible contributions through
primary-source examination, inspection of obtainable evidence, and purposeful
small probes. Writing this decomposition or a blank case template does not perform
that inquiry. Formation can be part of the proposed work even when its answers
are not yet available during decomposition.

The intended return is a checked comparison of candidate cases and a recommendation.
It identifies the problem and recipient, relevant mechanism, closest prior work
and possible addition, question and competing explanations or proposition,
observation/inference conditions, feasible evidence or argument, minimum means,
resources, scrutiny, and unresolved choices. Use the fields needed for the actual
research; descriptive, theoretical, and methodological work need not be forced
into an experimental-hypothesis template.

Make progression conditional on a sufficiently supported case and its actual
authority, not on the existence of a proposal. State how formation can return a
useful reframe or an evidenced lack of a feasible case. Preserve alternatives
without requiring an exhaustive survey, a fixed candidate count, or resolution
of every open branch. A bounded inquiry may already have this basis; reuse it.

## 2. Define bounded contributions

Partition by a coherent intended contribution and its examination needs. A useful
contribution may resolve a distinction, establish a measurement, test an account,
provide a justified analysis, or make a reusable operation possible. Questions,
phases, source documents, and code directories are not automatically separate
deliverables. Several questions can share one contribution, and one question
can require several contributions.

For each proposed contribution, make the following usable to its owner:

| Information | What it must explain |
|---|---|
| Purpose and coverage | Intended improvement or question, supported objectives, scope, exclusions, and source basis |
| Approach and progression | Plausible method or bounded alternatives; current formation/investigation/synthesis needs; conditions for dependent work; ordinary discretion and reserved choices |
| Inputs | Needed evidence, data, tools, permissions, or other results; intended use and suitability conditions; source or unresolved binding |
| Outputs | Anticipated usable results and their recipients; artifacts as means of communicating or enabling those results |
| Examination and improvement | What would establish competent work and support its findings; the review/critique route, repair responsibility, and how corrections will be checked |
| Ownership and integration | Responsible contribution, actual assignee if known, and who checks its fit with the whole, including new claims introduced by synthesis |
| Limits and completion | Relevant resources, failure/recovery conditions, and when to stop, retain an uncertainty, or reconsider the work |

Use stable references when others need to cite or revise contributions. Preserve
existing project IDs. If structured Packages and Deliverables are useful and no
convention exists, use flat `PKG-XX` groupings and coupled `DEL-XX-YY` IDs; add
groups only to make ownership, interfaces, or scope clearer. These references do
not certify compatibility with downstream setup. Avoid forcing a research topic
into one conceptual category merely to give its work an accountable home.

Size contributions so their basis, output, and examination remain coherent and
manageable under the actual conditions. Split work when it hides distinct
obligations or exceeds a useful scope. Keep connected repair and integration
with the owner where continuity serves the result. A deliverable is not a
promise that one agent invocation will complete it.

## 3. Bind examination to the claim

Identify what observations, derivations, comparisons, or other arguments could
support, weaken, or distinguish the proposed explanations. Specify the subject,
conditions, assumptions, intended inference, and important alternative accounts.
Do not invent a quantitative metric where an argument or substantive examination
better fits the contribution.

For empirical work, identify relevant sampling, controls, variability, exclusions,
and limits of generalization. For theoretical work, identify premises, inference
steps, applicability, and counterexamples or boundary cases to examine. For a
tool or method, distinguish correct operation from fitness for its research use.
Verification against a criterion and validation of that criterion's relevance
are separate questions; passing the former does not establish the latter.

Examine the observation and evaluation means themselves. Distinguish what an
actor or monitor can actually access from information reserved for evaluation.
Identify meaningful checks of extraction, measurements, labels, reference
answers, judges, and missing-record handling. Shared assumptions or agreement
among agents cannot supply independent support. Select independence appropriate
to the claim: another derivation, an independent source, withheld cases, or a
reviewer examining the actual evidence may expose different errors.

Record which decisions and analyses precede observation and which develop from
it. When exploratory findings motivate a subsequent test, preserve that relation;
do not present selected discovery cases as independent confirmation. Changes in
criteria, comparison conditions, or sample selection must remain visible.
Retain unsuccessful, interrupted, and inconclusive attempts where they bear on
the claim; explain exclusions rather than selecting only favorable evidence.

Define completion by the promised investigation and evidence quality. A negative
finding or supported insufficient-evidence conclusion may fulfill that promise.
An unperformed check, broken instrument, unavailable input, or exhausted budget
can instead leave it incomplete. Explain which applies; uncertainty is neither
automatic failure nor automatic completion. Research claims must be justified
from their actual principles and facts, not conformance to this method.

### Specify review, critique, repair, and backcheck

Every contribution needs an explicit arrangement for examining and improving
its results, proportionate to the claims and consequences involved. Identify
the candidate and evidence to examine, the questions a critic should challenge,
who provides scrutiny, who owns any repair, and what establishes that a finding
has been addressed. These can be stated in the contribution's existing brief.
Take standing research and review standards from the project's actual instructions
(such as its AGENTS.md), and case-specific criteria from the steering and selected
basis. The decomposition must make those conditions usable to the contribution
owner without creating a second governing copy of the instructions.

Plan examination of the assembled result as well as its parts, including new
inferences or lost qualifications introduced by synthesis. Distinguish author
checks, separate review, and independent evidence. Identify how supported defects,
mistaken objections, and unresolved concerns reach a warranted disposition under
the project's review practice. A smaller, better-supported conclusion can be an
improvement; a favorable finding is not an assumed completion condition.

## 4. Connect the contributions and their means

Trace actual consumer needs with their conditions, sources, and points of use.
Distinguish preliminary use from reliance on a checked result. Unknown bindings
remain explicit. Reciprocal learning can require iteration; do not invent an
acyclic graph or whole-deliverable finish-to-start rule. A missing declared edge
does not prove that work shares no data, resources, assumptions, or writes.

Explain the useful progression from formation and feasibility to focused
investigation with necessary support, then an integrated account. This is a
relationship among work and evidence conditions, not a compulsory phase taxonomy
or separate package for each phase. Sources, feasibility checks, and report
outlines can develop together; one case can progress while another remains open.
Identify the findings that would return affected work to formation, preserving
the original basis and continuing unaffected work. Use already-given decisions;
phase movement does not create a new approval requirement by itself.

Give shared evidence, tools, and methods an owning contribution and identifiable
consumers. Prefer existing suitable operations; a proposed capability should
serve selected work or a bounded feasibility inquiry. State its actual interface,
dependencies, effects, failure behavior, and limits before depending on it.
Instructions and metadata do not prove host enforcement. Qualify adapters on the
actual source surface and preserve a direct-reading or manual route where viable.

Assess relevant resource use, participant attention, delay, and repair alongside
research value. Unknown effort stays unknown. Collect only what examination,
reproduction, integration, or recovery needs. Search and structural checks can
expose gaps; retrieval similarity, file counts, and hashes cannot establish
semantic truth, complete domain coverage, or causal improvement.

Hand the resulting contributions to the project's selected orchestration practice.
Where `coordinated-knowledge-work` is selected, supply its owner with the input
basis, contribution boundaries, examination needs, shared conditions, return
route, and integration responsibilities. It carries coordinated execution and
repair; this method defines the research structure. Project instructions and
steering supply the research-specific standards and criteria. Preserve the
selected method's source identity and actual contract; naming a method does not
establish its prerequisites or start execution.

## 5. Examine and present the concrete decomposition

Check that adopted objectives and constraints are covered, options remain
distinguishable from commitments, shared work has an owner, and every proposed
contribution has a useful output and an adequate review, repair, and backcheck
route. Check the scrutiny of any new claims introduced by synthesis. Surface
unsupported assumptions, conflicts, duplicated ownership, and unresolved inputs
with the work they affect. Fix routine defects within authority.
Check that the proposal distinguishes readiness to form a question from readiness
to produce its evidence, and gives formation a concrete return and stopping point.

Walk a representative inquiry through the proposal, including a plausible
failure, contrary result, or interruption. Can its owner obtain the needed basis,
perform useful work, interpret the result honestly, and return something its
consumer can use? Arrange independent scrutiny for consequential assumptions or
claims where the governing instructions or evidence needs require it. State the
review's actual coverage; author self-checks are not independent examination.

Present one coherent proposal with its checks, open matters, tradeoffs, and next
use. The human reviews the concrete basis and contribution structure; no fixed
number of meetings or approvals is required by this method. Apply existing
decisions within their scope. Any acceptance must identify the content, accepted
portions, conditions, and actual decision, using the project's existing record
and revision mechanism. Preserve a recoverable version; hashes identify content
but do not manufacture a decision or validate its meaning.

Return the actual outcome: a usable proposal, an accepted decomposition, or a
partial result with precise outstanding conditions. A reviewed decomposition
has defined the research; it has not performed the experiments or established
their eventual conclusions.

## 6. Hand off, revise, and recover

The handoff supplies the identified basis, selected contributions, scope and
evidence conditions, input/use relationships, open decisions, and actual next
authorization. Detailed protocols and execution follow their own assignment.

Before handing to `project-setup` or another consumer, inspect its selected
contract and establish the mapping it requires. Familiar `PKG`/`DEL` IDs alone
do not establish compatibility. This workflow introduces no implemented
`RESEARCH` variant, schema, validator, or automatic setup route. Keep unsupported
mapping work explicit and resolve it before that dependent operation; ordinary
authorized investigations can still use adequate briefs and existing locations.

When findings change a question, assumption, method, or allocation, revise the
owning account and affected relationships. Preserve the earlier basis of
existing results and the reason for the change. Continue unaffected authorized
work. Refer changed commitments, reserved choices, and explicit holds to their
actual authority; do not reopen settled decisions merely because work moved.

On interruption, leave the current basis, usable work, checks, unresolved
conditions, and next useful action recoverable from their owning records. Add
one replaceable recovery note only if those records are insufficient. Resume by
inspecting actual state before replaying writes or relaunching an uncertain run.

The method is complete when the requested decomposition supports its intended
next use at its stated standing. Do not extend it into a standing research
program, automatic implementation, or unlimited search for additional questions.
Optional [walkthroughs](resources/walkthroughs.md) illustrate how to examine the
method; examples do not become project commitments or execution evidence.
