## Review independence and configuration

Both first-round reviewers assessed the candidate before reading the other's
return. Round 2 deliberately supplied both prior reviews and parent dispositions.
The Claude reviewer reports seeing a file-change notice with Codex's round-2
adoption status and the Last-Modified qualification, without reading that review
or the round-2 parent response. The process therefore does not claim fully blind
second-round assessment. It supplies separately prepared scrutiny and explicit
convergence, not independent empirical evidence for a research finding.

The dispatcher returned `claude-opus-5-5` with `effort=xhigh` and `gpt-6.1-sol`
with `reasoningEffort=xhigh` in both rounds. This confirms the requested settings
at dispatch; a child need not be able to introspect its own effort setting.

## Completion condition

Convergence requires a warranted common recommendation on the candidate and path
forward, with material objections resolved or explicitly brought to the owner.
No reviewer must agree merely to finish a round. After at most three review
rounds, remaining material disagreements go to the owner with the alternatives,
grounds, and consequences. The owner's eventual decision remains separate.
