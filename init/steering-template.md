# Research steering template

Use this optional aid after session orientation. Replace the prompts with the
actual direction, omit what is already established or irrelevant, and send it
in the conversation or explicitly ask the agent to read and execute the completed
file. This unfilled template selects no work.

## Intended contribution

[What should this undertaking help me understand or do? What usable result should
it return? This may be exploratory research, development of a research capability,
decomposition, or another bounded contribution.]

## Research position and next result

[Is this formation/feasibility, focused investigation, or synthesis/reporting?
What actual work establishes that position? For formation, request candidate case
briefs and a recommendation. For a later phase, identify the selected case and the
evidence/conditions that support the proposed next act.]

## Basis and scope

[Relevant project decisions, source material, existing work, questions to pursue,
and boundaries. Which directions are selected and which remain optional?]

## Approach and discretion

[Any chosen methods or starting case; what the agent may investigate, vary,
implement, and repair autonomously; what new findings should cause reconsideration.
Explain the multi-agent mechanism and oversight use. Leave the final conclusion
open where the investigation is meant to establish it.]

## Conditions for the work

[Actual data and tool access, permitted writes/actions, available resources and
limits, and any meaningful conditions on delegation or shared resources.]

## Examination and return

[Case-specific evidence needs, comparisons, review or independence requirements,
uncertainties to resolve or preserve, intended recipient/output location, and what
would make the contribution complete or require a return for direction.]

Apply `AGENTS.md`'s standing research and review standards. Use `research-decomp`
when the selected work needs decomposition, and `coordinated-knowledge-work` when
related contributions need coordination. The actual steering determines which
methods are needed; it need not repeat their full instructions.
