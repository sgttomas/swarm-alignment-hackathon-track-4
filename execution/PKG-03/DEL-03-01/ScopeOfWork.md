## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

The output is a coherent argument about the selected research, with explicit prior work, method, findings, limitations and implications. An outline/source account can develop early once authorized; case findings enter at their actual standing. Report preparation is distinct from external submission or publication.

The identified research basis and prior-work account, examined findings and relevant capability descriptions, actual review/correction consequences, current official template/event requirements and team details when their use requires them.

Anticipated outputs may share a coherent record; no fixed file per section is required. Work/ is the local home of evolving reasoning, evidence and returns. Tools/code or large/private/tool-constrained evidence can retain another appropriate home, referenced here. A path below is planned, not evidence of production.

- **OUT-001** — Integrated manuscript and grounds. Work/ contains or references the manuscript source and its inspectable claim/evidence, method, prior-work and review basis.
- **OUT-002** — Compliant report candidate. The actual rendered PDF and required title/abstract/authorship/appendix metadata are checked at a named revision; optional artifact links are included only if selected.
- **OUT-003** — Limitations and examined handoff. The record preserves material review dispositions, ethical/dual-use considerations, scientific unknowns, authorship responsibility and the actual candidate/return conditions.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. After activation, begin the outline and prior-work/source account alongside the selected inquiry; identify where a claim needs actual investigation rather than editorial completion.
2. Integrate findings at their demonstrated standing, critique new inferences and source relationships, and route underlying gaps to the responsible owner. Reserve participant drafting/revision time according to the actual chosen allocation.
3. Render and examine the report candidate and required appendix, repair and backcheck, and return the identified usable result and open conditions. External delivery follows actual owner direction.

Use the native input conditions in deliverable.yaml at their actual point of use, distinguishing preliminary use, later reliance and reciprocal feedback. Preserve source/criteria versions and real return routes. Apply the standing critique/repair/backcheck cycle without manufacturing a new approval at every step.

- **VER-001** — Trace central claims to actual source/evidence/argument and examine new synthesis inferences, shared premises, incompatible conditions, contrary findings and lost qualifications.
- **VER-002** — Inspect the actual rendered PDF, template and metadata; count pages/abstract words and file bytes, inspect required sections and supplied links, and state any unverified rendering or form limits.
- **VER-003** — Compare report/README attribution with actual source history, citations, assistance and selected drafting/revision allocation; do not infer personal-writing requirements from an encouragement.
- **VER-004** — Review the combined argument and required appendix, trace material findings through repair/backcheck, and check that incomplete work or narrow observations are not presented as broader established results.
- **VER-005** — Compare the handed-back candidate with the examined revision and required metadata/links; verify the current authority and hold without executing an external action.
