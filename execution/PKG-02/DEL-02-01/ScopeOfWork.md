# Scope of Work — DEL-02-01: Evidence access and inspection

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Provide a qualified route for a selected consumer to inspect evidence and its limitations, with faithful source binding and observation semantics.

The accepted project objective references are O2, O4; package purpose OBJ-002 is defined in decomposition §3. The accepted requirement slice is R14, R15, R20, R21. These labels refer to the PRD and decomposition, not claims of completed work.

Source basis: `docs/RESEARCH_DECOMP.md@3a73e382ac01d80bb3b1ac8790b88faeeecd9ae6`. [Actual owner decision](../../../docs/APPROVAL.md), [project instructions](../../../AGENTS.md), and [setup assignment](../../_Coordination/SETUP_BASIS.md) identify the scope, source and latest research hold.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

The output is an actual usable evidence access/inspection capability or a supported feasibility limitation at a named scope. It can reuse files, Git, public datasets, state queries or host exports; no universal event schema, complete host trace, Root service or dataset acquisition is presumed.

A consumer question and evidence distinction from case formation, an actual source/interface and permission to use it, and relevant identity/time semantics. The source account records access leads only; each consumed route needs its own qualification.

Anticipated outputs may share a coherent record; no fixed file per section is required. Work/ is the local home of evolving reasoning, evidence and returns. Tools/code or large/private/tool-constrained evidence can retain another appropriate home, referenced here. A path below is planned, not evidence of production.

- **OUT-001** — Qualified evidence route. Work/ records or references the actual source/interface, access/use basis, consumer purpose and observation/identity/time contract; code retains its maintained home.
- **OUT-002** — Inspectable evidence and views. Retained evidence, locators and any needed inspection view bind actual source/configuration, transformations and missing/ambiguous information; external tool-required homes remain external with references.
- **OUT-003** — Coverage and consumer-use qualification. The capability record identifies demonstrated behavior, limitations, failed feasibility, consumer input conditions and material review/repair outcomes.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. After research activation, qualify only the evidence surface needed by a selected question or bounded feasibility probe; preserve the actual source/access basis.
2. Use non-executing parsing and bounded inspection for untrusted payloads; treat embedded instructions/URLs as observations, preserve redactions, and retain evidence at its actual permitted home.
3. Examine representative valid and problematic inputs, document limits, critique and repair the route, and return the consumer-use conditions without claiming scientific conclusions.

Use the native input conditions in deliverable.yaml at their actual point of use, distinguishing preliminary use, later reliance and reciprocal feedback. Preserve source/criteria versions and real return routes. Apply the standing critique/repair/backcheck cycle without manufacturing a new approval at every step.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the actual source/interface and representative permitted read or failure, distinguishing endpoint/catalog visibility from acquired source contents and fitness.
- **VER-002** — Compare representative source material with its capture/transformation/view, including meaningful malformed, absent, stale or ambiguous inputs, and check the stated coverage.
- **VER-003** — Walk the intended consumer distinction against actual observations and counterexamples, examining time/order semantics and the limits of graph, hash or summary representations.
- **VER-004** — Exercise/inspect the relevant failure and recovery path, review scope and data handling, and trace repair to the saved route and its consumer qualification.
