## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

A selected route can reuse existing tools, scripts, calculations or examined reasoning. New trial generation is conditional on the case and actual grant. An operation working correctly is distinct from its measure being valid or its output supporting a causal claim.

The selected question/evaluation definition, suitable source/evidence bindings where consumed, an actual permitted environment and resources, and the consumer conditions for any reused tool. Root and Library names alone do not establish an integration.

Anticipated outputs may share a coherent record; no fixed file per section is required. Work/ is the local home of evolving reasoning, evidence and returns. Tools/code or large/private/tool-constrained evidence can retain another appropriate home, referenced here. A path below is planned, not evidence of production.

- **OUT-001** — Usable operation and interface. Work/ references the selected implementation or existing method, its consumer and actual input/output/side-effect/dependency contract.
- **OUT-002** — Bound results and correctness evidence. Selected configurations, inputs, criteria and outputs are identified with relevant executable or logical checks, including evaluator and information-access conditions.
- **OUT-003** — Use limitations and reproducibility account. The route exposes relevant failure/recovery behavior, available resource observations, reproducibility limits and consumer-use/review dispositions.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. After case activation, inspect suitable existing operations and add a component only for a justified gap. Record input/output, environment, actual grant and permitted side effects.
2. Bind selected execution/analysis results to case and criteria; preserve information-access separation, meaningful checks and relevant failed attempts.
3. Qualify correctness and fitness with their distinct grounds, critique/repair/backcheck, and return the actual consumer conditions and limits.

Use the native input conditions in deliverable.yaml at their actual point of use, distinguishing preliminary use, later reliance and reciprocal feedback. Preserve source/criteria versions and real return routes. Apply the standing critique/repair/backcheck cycle without manufacturing a new approval at every step.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the selected contract and exercise or substantively examine the operation against the intended input/output meaning and actual permitted environment.
- **VER-002** — Use appropriate known-answer, ordinary-success, failure and independent numerical/logical checks; inspect the oracle and review any untestable coverage explicitly.
- **VER-003** — Trace outputs to actual inputs/configuration/criteria and repeat or inspect the saved analysis as appropriate, including incomplete/failed attempts and the limits of stochastic replay.
- **VER-004** — Examine the actual case consumption and review/repair record, identify unsupported use, and verify that the saved corrected route and relevant consuming result agree.
