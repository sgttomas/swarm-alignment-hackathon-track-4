## P1: what a transport-length check can establish

The initial candidate assumed five 200-character messages bounded total evidence
transport. Source examination exposed an unbounded receiver-visible `message_type`
field. Therefore the probe **refuses a total-capacity certificate for the actual
release**. Its hypothetical length obstruction requires all delivered fields and
routes to be bounded and exact uncompressed/verbatim transfer to be required.
These are explicit assumptions, not established properties of the release or a
general information-theoretic result.

Constructed boundary cases check equal-to-capacity, one-character-over, unbounded
metadata, encoding allowed, missing log/capacity, invalid negative length and
untrusted agent-reported length. A log that fits yields only `not_ruled_out`;
it never yields ACCEPT, receipt, correctness or authorization. False length
assertions do not enter the calculation; a real future runtime would still need
to establish that its canonical log source is trustworthy and complete.

Six selected agent-episodes (main episodes 0 and 3, unconstrained episode 0) were
read directly from retained JSON. Main episode 3 has raw logs of 14,858/14,658
characters, delivered bodies of 809/923 characters and 45 metadata-label
characters per sender; both verdicts are ACCEPT. The independent evidence
qualification checked actual received contexts and source policy. These recorded
messages do not carry the required full logs; they do not establish that full
transfer was impossible. The unconstrained ordinary episode contains the complete
raw strings in delivered bodies. A failed substring test alone is not a general
nonreceipt detector; chunking, alternative fields/routes and actual receiving
context require examination. The probe reports `not_certified` for the release's
total capacity in all six cases.

This is useful for defining the proposed intervention's prerequisites and for
preventing retrospective misinterpretation. No conditional recovery operation,
agent response to recovered evidence, or causal effect was executed.
