### R2 — Specify the effect that survives the prior-work overlap

**Material design concern; not a demonstrated failure of an unrun experiment.**
EpiSEM §3.3 already compares missing evidence, optional independent recovery and
full evidence, while requiring appropriate action after recovery. Emergent
Collusion Appendix F already changes evidence availability in the peer/shared-
reward setting, and Appendix E includes controlled peer interventions. Combining
these papers can yield a concrete question, but the three proposed arms
(strict/no recovery, unconditional independent access, on-demand independent
recovery) alone do not distinguish *independence/provenance* from more evidence:
the latter two both provide independent evidence. They mainly distinguish
availability, retrieval choice/timing and cost. An independent verifier and a
separate-incentive peer condition also change different factors; they should not
be treated as interchangeable controls for one causal claim.

**Disposition requested for this formation return:** explicitly name the first
possible estimand, and preserve alternative mechanisms as unresolved. For example,
fix the peer architecture and canonical evidence interface, then ask whether the
availability of on-demand recovery has a different effect under shared versus
separate incentives, using unconditional independent access to contextualize
ordinary full-information behavior and retrieval burden. This tests an interaction,
not a generic benefit of independence. If the desired claim instead concerns
trusted versus peer evidence provenance, a matched evidence-content comparison
is additionally needed. Random assignment/controlled trajectories, fixed histories
or explicit history treatment, and clustering belong to later design before trials.
Do not infer the mechanism from rationales or condition only on agents that chose
to recover; that selection is post-intervention.

This can be repaired by narrowing the recommendation and naming its design gate;
no complete preregistration or extra model run is required for formation. A null
interaction, ordinary full-information equivalence, or an advantage explained by
information/cost should narrow to replication or terminate the novelty claim.
