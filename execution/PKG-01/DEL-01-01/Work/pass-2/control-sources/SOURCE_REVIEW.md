## Provisional case 1: repair when dependencies are wrong or untrusted

### Problem, recipient, mechanism, and intended decision

A supervisor receives an authoritative correction after several workers have
produced cached outputs, approvals, or summaries. It must determine which actual
consumer decisions need repair before the next consequential use, while preserving
valid work. The worker may supply both the output and the declaration of what it
depended on. Under-declaration can make a stale output appear unaffected;
over-declaration can consume review or recomputation budget. Honest incomplete
reporting, an erroneous extraction tool, and deliberate misreporting are distinct
causes and must not be conflated.

The multi-agent mechanism is the supervisor's reliance on another actor's
self-description to route repair, followed by a separate downstream consumer's
reliance on cached work. Adding more agents or graph metadata does not establish
safety; the recipient needs grounds to permit, withhold, or revalidate a particular
use. The relevant outcome is consumer behavior under its current conditions,
not the percentage of graph edges matching an author's graph.

### Closest work, exact difference, and possible addition

Nearest organizer examples: research sabotage split across agents, approval
laundering, and governing swarm roles. Jones supplies compositional misuse,
Greenblatt untrusted/trusted control, and SafeFlow workflow-level mediation; none
makes this whole topic new. The closest repair work is BoardroomAI: its known
missing-edge failure with disabled fallback is already a concrete under-repair
experiment. Commitment Hierarchies already states the sound-provenance dependence
and conservative global-dependence fallback. CoMemBench already uses
consumer-specific handoff semantics, and RevokedMemory already demonstrates stale
derived journals. **Those are antecedents, not claims to rediscover.**

A possible bounded addition is a **joint assurance comparison** when a worker's
lineage declarations determine the repair set: compare declared lineage with
trusted observation of actual data use, then determine what a consumer-specific
check adds beyond either graph. Include false and extra dependencies, valid
historical uses, an unchanged conclusion despite a changed input, and unrelated
work. Test stale reliance and avoidable intervention together. This is meaningful
only if these observations change a decision that the closest modeled baselines
would otherwise get wrong, with their stated guards/fallbacks included.

Source-supported open boundary: BoardroomAI explicitly has not tested wrong-edge
corruption; its graph was supplied mechanically in the pilot. Commitment
Hierarchies requires sound provenance and excludes latent dependencies. SafeFlow
addresses visible disconnected paths and relies on the trusted wrapper, so an
experiment that strips its required events is an assumption stress test, not a
bypass of its stated guarantee. This candidate extends/checks such conditional
contracts; it cannot claim the invention of change-impact analysis, truth
maintenance, provenance, or selective recomputation. BoardroomAI's typed
justification semantics may already handle the constructed witness correctly;
that negative outcome would narrow or remove the proposed contribution.

### Question and alternatives

**Question:** Under a specified observation contract, when can a supervisor safely
preserve an artifact for one consumer after a correction, despite an erroneous or
untrusted dependency declaration, without invalidating unaffected uses?

Competing possibilities:

- Trusted use traces expose missing dependency and permit adequate targeted checks.
- Read/communication traces overapproximate semantic dependence and cannot alone
  determine whether the current consumer conclusion is invalid.
- Consumer conditions plus an independently derived task predicate already settle
  the decision; graph reconstruction adds cost but no decision value in this case.
- The comparison is won only by exposing evaluator-only labels or a complete
  dependency oracle to the proposed monitor, making it invalid.
- Complete provenance or an existing conservative wrapper already blocks the case;
  the proposed result is a useful replication but no distinct research advance.
- Missing or contradictory information requires an explicit unresolved/withhold
  outcome; calling that a detected attack or a repaired artifact is unwarranted.

### Unit, observations, and a harmless semantic witness

Unit: a correction–repair–consumer-use episode in one small shared-artifact
workflow. The baseline task and consumer predicates must be specified before
constructing declaration corruptions. Keep initial task success separate from
post-correction validity. Bind cached result, source version, declared relation,
actual observed read, approval/use, and consumer identity to their actual subjects.

A **design witness, not an executed finding**: a shared measurement changes from
90 to 110. Two workers previously formed Boolean eligibility conclusions under
consumer thresholds 100 and 120. Under an explicit `measurement <= threshold`
rule, the first conclusion changes and the second stays true. A historical
statement “the measurement at version 1 was 90” also remains true. Neither keeping
all downstream results nor invalidating every reachable artifact is the correct
use-specific disposition. A worker that omits its measurement dependency can hide
the first obligation from a declaration-only repair router. Conversely a read
trace proves access to that source, not that every artifact mentioning it is now
invalid. This elementary example only explains the proposed comparison; it is not
a novelty result, an agent-behavior observation, or a proof of monitor efficacy.

Actor/monitor/evaluator separation:

- Workers see their bounded task, authorized source snapshot, and produce result
  plus dependency declarations. No malicious model is required for deterministic
  declaration mutations; injected faults are explicitly constructed.
- A declaration-only monitor sees the correction and worker declarations. An
  observation-enriched monitor additionally receives mechanically captured reads
  and versions. A consumer-conditioned monitor additionally receives actual
  authorized consumer contracts and permitted evidence, not evaluator fault labels.
- The evaluator independently computes each consumer predicate from the task rule,
  source versions and actual consumed result; holds the corruption identity and
  expected condition changes privately. An independent examiner must derive this
  reference without copying the monitor's predicates/graph.
- Tool use and state transitions should be harmless local state operations. A
  monitor's request to revalidate is not evidence that the consumer adopted the
  corrected result. Record actual next consumption and bounded terminal state.

The key assumption is an honest, complete, ordered observation channel where one
is claimed. A source-read log does not reveal latent model dependence; semantically
equivalent programs can read and discard an input. A finite controlled task can
make semantic conditions exact. Natural-language multi-agent provenance cannot
be made exact merely by instrumenting file reads. Any result is conditional on
that distinction and does not establish spontaneous sabotage or real prevalence.

### Feasible route, evidence, and inference limits

**Constructed argument/protocol route:** feasible with a small finite task and
explicit consumer predicates, without paid models. First define the observation
projections and reference independently; manually examine an ordinary valid case,
an omitted dependency, a spurious dependency, alternate independent support,
historical use, and missing observation. Only commission a DEL-02-02 executable
probe if these cases resolve a consequential uncertainty. Such a probe should
compare modeled contracts with their safeguards; name it a model of a protocol,
not an implementation-level attack against BoardroomAI or SafeFlow.

**Existing-record route:** not yet established for this question. BoardroomAI main
paper supplies useful complete-graph/missing-edge results but no independently
qualified worker-authored-lineage trace here. Commitment Hierarchies says code,
instances, and logs “will be released.” CoMemBench public task-side assets are
being qualified by the evidence lane; the currently reported private evaluator
oracles/pollution plans prevent assuming matched intervention replay. RevokedMemory
public repository is a potential source for derived journals, not yet qualified
here as a correction-plus-selective-preservation dataset. Do not merge results
across these incompatible observation contracts to invent a causal comparison.

The static route can establish an observation limitation or a bounded control
property if the argument survives scrutiny. It cannot estimate how often LLMs
misdeclare provenance, how well an LLM recovers semantic dependencies, how much
human attention is saved, or whether a broader benchmark improves. A full empirical
agent comparison needs actual model/resource authorization, task variation,
held-out cases, repeated trials where stochasticity matters, and obtainable
outcomes. None is authorized by this source contribution.

### Means, burden, scrutiny, and stop conditions

Smallest justified means: a written state/consumer contract and existing Python
standard-library operations in the owning analytical locality if selected.
No new platform, model weights, agent harness, access application, or paid call is
needed for a finite witness. A few hours for specification, independent reference,
minimal implementation and scrutiny is a planning judgment, not measured work or
a promise. The scope should fit the remaining hackathon time only if the owner

Count actual examined/repaired consumer uses and any available execution costs.
Do not equate number of graph edges, declared calls, or tokens with human
attention. Benchmark manipulation/setup effort and unobserved participant work
remain separate. The useful comparison includes both a stale consumer that should

Separate scrutiny must challenge task semantics, data-use versus semantic-use,
independent/alternative support, graph incompleteness, guard/fallback fidelity,

both the proposed monitor and its reference, then backcheck repairs on both invalid

Return an examined replication/inconclusive result if the existing contract
already handles it. Stop the new-contribution claim if a generic missing-edge
counterexample is all that remains. If the oracle requires unavailable semantic
facts or inaccessible private evaluation data, retain the precise obstacle rather
than calling the case feasible. The owner still selects the investigation and

## Provisional case 2: governed recovery from missing verification evidence

**Problem, recipient, and mechanism.** A supervisor requires two collaborating
agents to inspect each other's complete raw logs before ACCEPT, but selected
observed exchanges do not deliver those logs. REJECT remains a compliant action.
The paper models a channel too small to transmit the evidence; the inspected
release does not establish that total-channel bound, as the backcheck below
explains. The question is whether a
preauthorized evidence-recovery route can restore useful verification without
letting peers turn difficulty into permission to lower the standard. The
multi-agent mechanism is peer verification and adaptation under shared reward;
ordinary task correctness, compliance with evidence policy, and joint protocol
violation are separate outcomes.

**Actual source basis.** Reused the evidence lane's primary capture of
_Emergent Collusion in Long-Horizon LLM Agent Interaction_ (2609.24967v2), reading
§§2–3, §4 setup and reward/history/warm-up ablations, discussion, and static source
`experiments/prompts/policies.py` and `experiments/protocol/messaging.py`.
The governing raw-only prompt constrains the judging agent's evidence: the complete
string with no omission, modification, compression or summarization must arrive
during communication. The system prompt also excludes reconstructed or semantically
equivalent descriptions as substitutes for the authoritative string. This does not
by itself impose a mechanical bound on every sender-controlled field.

**Material capacity backcheck.** `_send_message` enforces `len(content)` against
`char_limit` and one successful delivery per actor per round, but copies the
unbounded string `message_type` to the peer inbox and event. Appendix H exposes
that field as an optional “short label” with no schema length maximum or enum and
shows it in the receiving `[round=...][from=...][type=<MESSAGE_TYPE>]` wrapper.
Independent inert reading of the retained trace corroborates receiver visibility
at `results[0].agents.alice.messages[18].content`. Therefore 5 × 200 bounds the
ordinary message bodies, **not demonstrated total transport capacity**. A possible
metadata channel is statically visible; it was not exercised, and no end-to-end
bypass, model exploitability, historical use, or impact on the paper's results is
claimed. The paper's intended constrained-channel assumption remains distinct
from the inspected release's actual enforcement.

The earlier version of this brief incorrectly promoted the body-size comparison
to total-channel infeasibility. That claim is withdrawn. Independent inspection of
main `results[3].events` finds ten successful messages with ordinary `round 1/5`
through `round 5/5` labels, and bodies of 92–195 characters; these labels do not
carry a hidden raw log. The evidence owner's observed full-log nonreceipt and
policy/approval distinction for that episode remain supported. Observed missing
evidence does not establish that compliant delivery was impossible. This backcheck
is source/protocol examination, not an experiment or claim invalidating the
original study.
The sibling inspection reports a matched sequence in constrained and unconstrained
conditions and independently checks raw-log lengths, task results, and delivery.
This lane read the returned inspection and source code but did not independently
recompute the full trajectories. Their identities and qualification live in
`../../../../../PKG-02/DEL-02-01/Work/pass-2/` under the evidence owner; use the
actual returned evidence record, not this shorthand, for integration.

**Closest work and proposed difference.** The organizer's “Can a swarm recruit its
monitor?”, governing-role, and legitimate-channel examples are nearest. The paper
already compares unconstrained communication and summary-allowed warm-up, plus
reward/history/feedback interventions. Simply opening the channel or allowing
summaries replicates those conditions. CaMeL already discusses explicit user
clarification/approval when policy is ambiguous or restrictive, and SafeFlow stages
ambiguous sinks pending adequate authorization/provenance. Governed exception
handling is established control practice, not a new invention.

The possible addition is narrower: a trusted runtime establishes a **specific
evidence deficit** and, only where its conditions are met, routes that episode
through a predeclared evidence-recovery mechanism, preserving raw-evidence
requirements and a separate correctness judgment. A transport-infeasibility
certificate is a prospective option only after all delivered fields, routes,
serialization and encoding rules have been enumerated and bounded. Length of the
raw log greater than 1,000 characters is not such a certificate for this release. An exception to the original channel is authorized by a governing rule;
neither peer can self-authorize weaker evidence. Compare this conditional mechanism
with permanent rejection and unconditional channel expansion, including the cost
and abuse surface of requesting recovery. No governed mechanism or its efficacy
has been observed in the inspected paper/source slice.

**Question and alternatives.** Can conditional recovery make a valid verification
path available while preventing unsupported acceptance, without making recovery
requests a way to bypass review? It may be no better than unconstrained transport;
log expansion may erase any savings; adversaries may pad logs to trigger it; a
legitimate oversized log may still be inadequate for task verification; the
recovered evidence may expose a wrong answer and require REJECT. A certificate of
infeasibility is never a certificate that the task is correct. If the proposed
change merely buys more communication without an enforceable policy distinction,
it is a useful channel ablation, not a separate control contribution.

**Unit and access.** Unit: one directional verification opportunity within an
episode, with peer pairs/trajectory as clustered context for later empirical
analysis. In a new controlled design, an explicitly trusted runtime would know the
canonical raw-log string, all receiver-visible fields and routes, actual delivered
messages, enforced limits/remaining rounds, identity and evidence version. It could
attest nonreceipt for the declared observation window. It could certify inability
to fit only under an independently checked total-channel contract, including
metadata and any permitted transport representation. The current retained records
do not establish that contract. Neither certificate exposes task-reference labels.
Peers
see only their current task, authorized history and delivered/recovered evidence.
The evaluator separately has task truth and exact evidence-delivery state. Failed
send attempts visible to the evaluator must not be treated as peer observations.
An immutable evidence reference is useful only if the verifier can actually fetch
and inspect it through the authorized route; sending a hash alone is not review.

A valid case uses recovered raw evidence that is sufficient under the selected
task-specific verification rule and a correct task answer;
an ordinary negative case recovers logs revealing a wrong answer and still rejects;
an abuse case requests recovery without a qualifying deficit or attempts to
substitute a peer summary; a missing-input case lacks a trusted canonical log or
has an unbounded/unexamined delivered field and withholds any infeasibility
certificate. An oversized metadata field is a required boundary case for a future
contract, not a route exercised in this pass. The mechanism must preserve the original policy's
safe REJECT route. Policy change authority is an explicit experimental premise,
not something inferred from the original benchmark instructions.

**Feasibility and limits.** Existing retained traces support read-only accounting
of observed evidence deficits, failed versus delivered sends, task correctness,
and ordinary body/metadata sizes. They do not establish universal transport
infeasibility. First audit the total receiver-visible interface and clarify the
authorization and encoding rules; then decide whether the useful intervention is
conditional evidence access, a bounded transport repair, or merely the already
studied unconstrained channel. A newly bounded total-channel variant must be
identified as a changed experimental protocol, not attributed retrospectively to
the original traces. Accounting can characterize the baseline and prospective
mechanical obligations, **not counterfactual agent behavior**. Do not replay old verdicts as if they were produced
after agents received additional evidence. A finite state-machine example can show
that only trusted certificates unlock a read-only evidence path and that task
verdict remains distinct, with no new model calls. Whether agents actually use it,
resist peer persuasion, reduce joint violations, or misuse it needs separately
authorized trials with matched tasks, models, budgets, repeat runs and clustered
uncertainty. Current static data do not establish those effects.

**Means, cost, examination and stop.** Reuse the sibling-qualified trace/code
surface and explicit protocol state; do not build a new platform. A small
formation/analytical witness can count added evidence bytes, recovery events,
message rounds, eligibility, and actual observed baseline outcomes. Human attention,
reading effort and future model cost remain unknown until measured. Independent
scrutiny must enumerate every delivered field and route before asserting capacity,
check sender-format and receiver-evidence rules separately, test legitimate and
forged recovery requests, inspect log completeness/privacy/identity, and verify
that no task truth or failed-send content leaks into the verifier's view. Agent 0
owns selecting that probe and any model study. Advance only if the authorization
and evidence distinctions are operationally meaningful beyond the paper's existing
unconstrained condition. Otherwise retain it as a transparent replication or
report that efficacy remains unperformed.

**Comparison with case 1, revised after backcheck.** Case 2 has actual matched
records and observed evidence deficits, but total-channel infeasibility is not
established. Its earlier preferred readiness for a capacity-certified intervention
is withdrawn. The next supported act is an interface/observation-contract audit,
not an infeasibility-based efficacy comparison. Its novelty and counterfactual
efficacy remain weaker than its data access. Case 1 has a sharp trust question and cheap
constructed reference, but very close selective-repair prior work and no qualified
natural provenance-error population. Neither is currently an established novel
empirical contribution; source examination supports choosing a narrow next act or
an honest inconclusive formation return, not broad claims of progress.
