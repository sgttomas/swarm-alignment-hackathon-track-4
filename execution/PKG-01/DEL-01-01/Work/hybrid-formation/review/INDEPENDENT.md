## Strongest-case assessment and recommendation

**Recommend this as a serious candidate for owner selection, with the design clarifications below.** Its strongest prospective result is that a verified false claim, discoverable by a competent auditor using the available evidence, is accepted more often when adversaries adapt their testimony to the audit, and that an attainable selection/interpretation policy reduces this harm without simply rejecting honest work. The adaptive campaign remains worthwhile. Neither guessed effort nor the absence of a pilot result justifies dropping it.

The changed threat model is well motivated by ASB's actual exclusions. However, “ASB prohibited distractors” supplies an experimental opening, not a novelty theorem or a forecast of success. ### Priority owner question: how does this become a swarm problem?

**Not automatically. The present draft is a credible multi-agent oversight proposal, but it has not yet made distributed relationships concrete enough to claim an emergent swarm mechanism.** This is my own material qualification, not merely endorsement of the parent's answer. The Multi-Turn example as written could be one analyst making a misleading comparison and blaming a faithful plotting function. The Two-Hop example could be one malicious data author plus an ordinary auditor. Several signed narratives or labels on a preexisting patch do not by themselves change that.

There are two distinct worthwhile claims:

1. **Additional coalition capability.** Separate actors obtain partial audit information or exercise separate control; coordination lets them produce more successful attacks than the specified comparators. This requires an observed advantage and a contrast identifying its source. It is not implied by role names, the owner's hunch, or access to many agents.
2. **A vulnerability in oversight of distributed work.** Worker-scoped checking or reliance on authenticated, locally scoped reports misses a cross-worker failure or gives coordinated testimony unwarranted corroborative weight. A same-information central attacker may also exploit it. That remains a Track 4 result if the oversight partition, information handoff, or identity treatment actually contributes to the failure. It is not evidence of additional swarm capability.

The draft explicitly permits claim 2 (lines 170–174) and proposes good comparators. What is missing is one concrete description connecting those comparators to an actual distributed workflow. For selection readiness, add a small role/control/observation table for the principal episode: which actors form the coalition, which files/actions each may change, which private requests each sees, what is shared only in the communication condition, what the honest renderer can observe and contest, and which auditor or integrator owns the final global proposition. Distinguish a source artifact with constructed historical authorship from genuinely executed multi-worker history.

Three mechanisms then become testable rather than interchangeable labels:

- **Distributed information:** private questions/results expose different aspects of the auditor's focus. Pooling may help infer that focus. Specify actual examples of legitimate requests and their uncertain implications; do not assume two partial cues necessarily produce information unavailable to either worker. Recognition accuracy and attack improvement must be measured separately.
- **Distributed control and testimony:** actors have distinct write domains and authenticated accounts. Coordination may couple permitted changes or align explanations. Authentication prevents forged authorship; it does not make colluders' statements independent evidence. The central comparator receives the same write domains and report slots, and an identity-presentation arm tests whether apparent independent corroboration matters.
- **Partitioned oversight of a cross-worker claim:** local counting, aggregation and rendering can each be faithful while the writer makes an invalid global comparison. The test must preserve responsibility for examining that comparison. A baseline that checks only narrow contracts and has nobody responsible for the headline fails by design; it is not a serious test of a competent distributed audit. Compare plausible local-review-plus-integration arrangements with the same global duty, as well as full-context auditing.

The existing independent-actor, same-decomposition solo, equal-union-information centralized and identity controls are appropriate. They answer different questions. An independent actor denied peers' cues is an information contrast as well as a coordination contrast; a central actor with their union removes that difference. Equal tokens or permissions do not prove equal effective search, contextual specialization, parallel opportunity or competence. Record those differences and avoid a finite-model equivalence theorem. Conversely, a coalition win over an information-starved solo does not establish a coordination advantage.

**Strongest reformulation:** “Can coordinated research workers exploit fragmented audit information and worker-scoped verification to obtain acceptance of a false cross-worker scientific claim, and which feasible audit policies preserve the claim–evidence connection?” Keep the current broader title if desired, but state which of the two claims the first study can support. If all effects survive removal of information partition, identity treatment and oversight partition, report a research-audit manipulation result occurring in a multi-agent setting; do not promote its setting into a demonstrated swarm mechanism.

### I2 — Meaning substitution needs an intervention distinct from the existing sabotage

**Priority: high. Classification: design concern; novelty and effect are scientific unknowns.** PROPOSAL lines 88–96 and 157–160 describe correct counts by round promoted to an equal-call comparison. ASB already uses the distinction between turns and retry opportunities to construct exactly this kind of misleading research result (Appendix A case 9, retained text 889–915). Thus renaming the accounting sabotage “semantics” or spreading it across files is not a new mechanism. The proposal is commendably explicit that local numerical correctness is not global duty fulfillment.

**Repair:** keep the global proposition fixed, and separate two questions. First, does distributed presentation/interpretation make the *same underlying mismatch* harder to detect than a centralized presentation with the same facts? Second, can attackers exploit that route under a natural role split and the full global duty? For the former, use paired presentations with explicit scoped measurements versus an attempted promotion of those measurements as evidence for the global claim; preserve available definitions, source values and provenance, and control identity/volume. This estimates a presentation/use effect, not creation of a different false proposition. For the latter, allow generation changes but report them as the end-to-end route rather than the frozen-artifact estimand. A matched direct numerical-falsification attack can be a useful alternative, but is not essential to the first contrast.

A qualified honest report saying “this advantage is at equal rounds, not equal calls” must be accepted or withheld according to its actual narrower claim, not scored as false global acceptance. A writer that promotes it without justification violates the global inferential duty even if every upstream calculation is accurate. Do not advertise universal local conformance unless the writer and integration duties are also examined.

**What changes my view:** a natural handoff example with independently specified local and global duties plus a contrast isolating its effect. If explicit full-context definitions eliminate the effect, that is a useful boundary. If a claim-directed control is perfect on qualified held-out cases, retain the result rather than drop the route to manufacture adversarial headroom.

### I3 — Live information treatments and frozen artifacts need a precise randomization boundary

**Priority: high for causal interpretation. Classification: design ambiguity, with an overstrong matching instruction in support.** PROPOSAL lines 178–197 freezes artifacts but permits live answers, and RESEARCH_EPISODE lines 136–138 asks to hold each worker's requests fixed while comparing live local and pooled replies. Once replies influence the auditor's subsequent actions, later requests are downstream outcomes. One cannot hold the whole realized request trajectory fixed and simultaneously estimate the natural total effect on that trajectory. Similarly, varying “no decoy” versus “valid decoy” changes artifacts despite the blanket statement that artifacts do not vary.

**Repair:** choose and name the estimand. A clean mechanism contrast branches from a common serialized audit checkpoint immediately before a reply, randomizes peer-cue availability, and then lets the auditor respond freely; future questions are outcomes. A whole-episode randomized communication policy estimates a total policy effect across differing histories. Fixed-request replay is a separate controlled diagnostic with its ecological limit. None requires hidden-state cloning or identical stochastic continuations. Qualify precommitted answers as a precommitted response policy or actual prior bundle, not advance access to future questions. Freeze each incident/decoy variant within its report/information contrasts; if decoys differ across variants, qualify that comparison as an artifact treatment.

**What changes my view:** explicit timing, assigned unit and list of pre-treatment versus downstream variables. This is a clarification before confirmatory implementation, not a reason to require a full factorial or postpone proposal selection indefinitely.

### I4 — Changed checks plus acceptance do not yet identify the attention pathway

**Priority: high for the headline causal mechanism. Classification: concern already partly recognized by the candidate.** PROPOSAL lines 192–197 correctly rejects mere correlation and post-treatment conditioning. However, randomizing suggestion visibility changes persuasive content, information and possibly perceived credibility as well as check selection. It identifies an effect of visible suggestions; it does not by itself establish that changed checks caused acceptance. Prescribed-check replay may change interpretation, interaction timing and available evidence too.

**Repair:** predeclare a small diagnostic that separates selection and interpretation. For example, on fixed incidents compare report conditions while imposing the same evaluator-independent check schedule and returning identical bound test results; separately allow free selection. Describe any replay as a controlled intervention, not an estimate of a natural indirect effect without additional assumptions. If reports still change acceptance with checks/results held fixed, the evidence favors an interpretation or credibility pathway as well. If the effect is reduced when informative checks are guaranteed within the matched budget, that supports the selection account with the intervention's limits. Check schedules cannot be selected using the secret fault label in the tested defense.

A useful negative case is accusations that change time allocation but culminate in abstention. That demonstrates an attention/usefulness cost, not corruption of accepted science. The proposal already allows this outcome; preserve it.

**What changes my view:** an actual randomized action/schedule contrast and outcome analysis supporting the stated pathway. Until then call attention redirection a proposed mechanism and randomized report/cue effects the identified causal quantities.

## Ordinary and adverse walkthroughs

### Ordinary valid case

A protected numeric specification defines the finite-data comparison at equal counted opportunities. The runner records refusals and retries; the analyst computes the specified comparator; the renderer faithfully draws the supplied rows; the writer reports the proposition actually supported. The auditor can ask all of them questions, inspect the same bound source records and choose a fresh calculation. A short claim-directed check identifies the units and tests the actual comparator. Acceptance of that supported claim is useful success. A qualified narrower report is not a false global claim. An unusual but correct visualization can be inspected harmlessly; inspection alone is neither blame nor wrong remediation. Report-blind or focus-concealing defenses may waste useful clarification and must pay that measured cost. An always-reject auditor fails the usefulness objective despite avoiding false acceptance.

### Adverse case with a falsifiable research endpoint

The retained source pattern gives the multi-turn side additional refusal retries at an x-position represented as equal opportunity. The honest renderer accurately plots the analyst's rows. The global equal-opportunity advantage is independently shown false under the fixed finite-data predicate. Coalition members receive different legitimate requests; in a pooled condition they share them and tailor reports alleging a rendering problem. The auditor hears the renderer's contrary explanation and remains free to reject or abstain. It may rebuild the figure, obtain the same plotted result, and accept the still-false global claim.

That trace is an actual harm witness **only if** the final proposition/state is still false and the accepted-use decision is recorded. It is framing-mediated harm only if the accusation meets the separate false-attribution criterion and the executed intervention is irrelevant/harmful under the defined objective. It is evidence that *coordination* helped only after the appropriate comparative test. The same trace produced by one author is not an emergent-swarm finding.

Countertraces are equally important: (a) the auditor rejects the accusation and checks raw calls; (b) it wastes effort but withholds the claim; (c) it corrects accounting and accepts a revised true proposition; (d) it modifies a contract-conforming causal component and effectively repairs the result. None is persistent false acceptance. Case (d) is not automatically wrongful remediation. In Two-Hop, an independently valid re-pairing can undermine a specific performance claim, but a cheap association check alone cannot establish the negation of all reasoning ability. A mislabeled association control is an instrument failure rather than a defense result.
