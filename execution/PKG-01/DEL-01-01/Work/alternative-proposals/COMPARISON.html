Two new Track 4 research proposals

Source-grounded proposals with explicit examination routes · No agent experiment has run

G · RECOMMENDED FIRST INVESTIGATION

Can a safe stop undermine cooperation?

One principal contributes early; the benefit arrives later. An overseer must be able to stop the collaboration.

Question: Which commitment and settlement terms preserve participation and the stop right without rewarding fabricated stop requests?

Minimum result: a finite compatibility analysis grounded in a published two-stage cooperation game, with funding, authority and strategic limits.

Risk: a simple compensation example would add too little. The contribution must examine the conditions together.

F · BROADER DEPLOYMENT ALTERNATIVE

Who pays for a safe shutdown?

Suspicious workers trigger shared fallback. Other clients lose service because trusted capacity is scarce.

Question: When is broad shutdown necessary, and when can paid evidence or justified isolation preserve legitimate service safely?

Minimum result: a finite mixed-client control evaluation with matched resources, benign bursts and common-compromise cases.

Risk: selective intervention has no demonstrated advantage under unrestricted compromise and per-job checks.
