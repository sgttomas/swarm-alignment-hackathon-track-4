## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

The contribution is the examined research basis and case definition, not the mere existence of a topic list. The initial work is formation and feasibility under accepted decomposition §6.1. Each case identifies the multi-agent mechanism, intended oversight use, closest organizer example and primary work, proposed addition or useful replication, competing explanations/proposition, observation/inference conditions, feasibility, minimum means, resource limits and scrutiny.

Owner steering, the accepted PRD/decomposition, all four event tabs and their retained source account, relevant primary work, and bounded access/observation checks when the candidate needs them. Availability metadata identifies leads rather than qualified scientific evidence. Exact current input/use conditions live in deliverable.yaml.

Anticipated outputs may share a coherent record; no fixed file per section is required. Work/ is the local home of evolving reasoning, evidence and returns. Tools/code or large/private/tool-constrained evidence can retain another appropriate home, referenced here. A path below is planned, not evidence of production.

- **OUT-001** — Research basis. Work/RESEARCH_BASIS.md records examined sources, definitions, candidate questions, prior-work relationships, and actual evidence/feasibility observations.
- **OUT-002** — Candidate briefs and recommendation. Sections of the same basis or linked case records state proposed investigations, comparisons, alternatives, means, limits, and the justified next choice or supported lack of a feasible case.
- **OUT-003** — Critique and unresolved choices. The owning basis retains material objections, responses, backchecks, actual unknowns, and the next useful act without presenting incomplete inquiry as completed research.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. After research activation, inspect the selected sources and obtainable evidence using the current case/resource bounds; use small probes only for named feasibility questions.
2. Develop and compare case briefs, including the closest examples, prior work, actual multi-agent mechanism, discriminating evidence and feasible alternatives.
3. Critique the candidates, repair supported weaknesses, and return a bounded recommendation or precise obstacle. Revisit only affected assumptions and preserve the basis of earlier observations.

Use the native input conditions in deliverable.yaml at their actual point of use, distinguishing preliminary use, later reliance and reciprocal feedback. Preserve source/criteria versions and real return routes. Apply the standing critique/repair/backcheck cycle without manufacturing a new approval at every step.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the cited primary passages and case account for fidelity, applicability and the difference between an organizer example, a source statement, and a supported proposed addition.
- **VER-002** — Inspect every serious case brief against AC-002's required fields and conditions for completeness, then walk a representative candidate through its proposed evidence or argument and a material missing-input or competing-explanation case; confirm that the inference and required conditions fit.
- **VER-003** — Critique the comparison against counterevidence, inaccessible data, weak references, faulty evaluation and practical scope; check that the actual effort bound did not become evidence of scientific completion.
- **VER-004** — Trace material findings to responses and actual corrections/backchecks, identify scrutiny independence, and verify that the next return does not clear the research hold or invent a case selection.
