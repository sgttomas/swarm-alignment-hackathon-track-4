## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. After activation and case qualification, carry out the selected evidence/argument route using the actual allowed resources and input/use conditions.
2. Analyze findings and competing explanations; preserve source/configuration identity, selection and failure history, and material unknowns.
3. Review, repair and backcheck before returning findings for synthesis. If a premise fails, return the affected framing/means issue to its owner while preserving unaffected work.

Use the native input conditions in deliverable.yaml at their actual point of use, distinguishing preliminary use, later reliance and reciprocal feedback. Preserve source/criteria versions and real return routes. Apply the standing critique/repair/backcheck cycle without manufacturing a new approval at every step.

- **VER-001** — Compare the case record with the selected brief and actual input/criteria versions, including what changed after observations and what resource/authority conditions applied.
- **VER-002** — Inspect or reproduce relevant analysis/derivation against actual sources and meaningful known/contrary cases; obtain independent numerical/logical scrutiny where the claim requires it.
- **VER-003** — Critique the actual inference and selection history, compare rival accounts, and check that absence of a detected effect, missing records, or agreement among agents is not overinterpreted.
- **VER-004** — Backcheck the corrected result and trace changed meaning to the report consumer or pending handoff; reuse unaffected evidence and identify remaining scrutiny or execution gaps.
